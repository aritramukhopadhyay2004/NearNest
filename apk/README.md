### Installation Instructions
1. Tap the direct download link above from your Android smartphone browser.
2. Once the download completes, tap the notification or locate `NearNest-latest.apk` in your **Downloads** folder.
3. If prompted with *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*:
   - Tap **Settings**.
   - Enable **Allow from this source**.
4. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.
5. Grant Location and (optional) Microphone permissions to start real-time tracking and using the AI Movement Assistant.
